Kenny´s Tuner

A quick guide to everything it does.

Tuner

Tap Start tuner, allow the microphone, and play a note. The needle shows if you're sharp or flat; the big letter is the detected note and turns green when you're in tune.

Instrument & tuning

Pick your instrument from the tabs, then choose a tuning from the dropdown (Standard, Drop D, DADGAD, and more). Choose Custom… to set each string yourself — the strings blink until you've picked a note for every one.

Reference pitch

The toggle in the top-right switches between 440hz (standard) and 432hz. Everything retunes to the chosen reference.

Spectrum

A live view of everything the microphone hears, scrolling by in real time. Each note has its own line; the notes of your selected tuning are marked by the bright letters on the left.

Tune through noise. Because Spectrum shows the true pitch of every sound, you can tune by watching where your note's line sits — even in a loud, noisy room. The needle can be thrown off by background sound, but in Spectrum you can pick your string's line out of the noise.

Spectrum knobs

Resolution sharpens pitch detail. Sensitivity sets how quiet a sound still registers. Noise Filter strips away background hiss so only clear notes show.

Offline

Add it to your home screen to use it like a real app — it works fully offline once installed.
